Privacy Policy

World of Trade · last updated 21 August 2026

In short: World of Trade does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. Everything the game saves stays on your own device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party services.

What the game stores

World of Trade saves your career progress locally, using your browser's local storage or the app's private storage. This includes your trading house and trader name as you type them, game progress (cash, positions, reputation, experience level, offices, history) and your settings.

This data never leaves your device. It is not uploaded or shared with anyone. There are no servers.

What the game does not do

  • No user accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access.
  • No file access beyond a file you explicitly choose when importing or exporting a career backup.
  • No data sold or shared with third parties.

Permissions in the Android app

  • Internet — required by Android for the app's internal web view. The game is bundled inside the app and plays fully offline; it makes no network requests.
  • Vibration — haptic feedback on button presses and important events. It can be switched off in the game's settings.

Exporting and importing your career

You can export your career as a JSON file and import it later. The file is written to a location you choose through your device's own file picker.

Deleting your data

Starting a new career or clearing a save slot erases that data. Uninstalling the app, or clearing your browser's site data, removes everything the game stored.

Children

World of Trade is a business strategy game with no chat, no user-generated content and no data collection. It is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts collecting data — for example if online leaderboards are added — this policy will be updated before that feature ships.
