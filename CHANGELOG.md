# World of Trade v33 — Global Risks & Progressive Expansion

## Major changes

- New career starts as a small Geneva commodity trading company with $500K cash, a $1.5M credit line, one commodity (copper), one supplier (Baltic Metals OU in Tallinn) and one customer (Lombardia Cables in Brescia).
- Commodities and countries now unlock by player level. Commodity-rich and capital-intensive origins unlock later.
- Added an enterprise risk engine covering geopolitical, macroeconomic, weather, operational, credit, FX, regulatory and compliance shocks.
- Risk events affect expected and realized P&L through price moves, freight, delays, financing rates, credit capacity, equity requirements, acceptance and direct firm costs.
- Added a permanent event P&L ledger and risk-category radar.
- Added expansion-stage roadmap and level requirements on locked opportunities and hubs.
- Redesigned navigation into vertical left and right rails.
- P&L, NAV, cash, credit, risk and reputation are always visible in a permanent right-side metric rail.
- Increased text sizes throughout the interface.
- Save format upgraded to `wot-v33`, with automatic migration from v32 and earlier.
